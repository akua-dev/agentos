## Give First Mate a boundary, not a graph

Use the real outcome from the previous chapter. State what should become true, the evidence that
will prove it, the repository or operational boundary and the authority that remains yours. Leave
the internal Agent choreography open.

> Add server-side validation to onboarding and deliver it through this repository's existing pull
> request checks. Preserve the public API. Do not merge.

You supplied the acceptance boundary and excluded authority. You did not decide in advance how
many workers exist, whether the current endpoint already exposes the needed field or which test
will reveal the cause.

## Watch current reality shape the work

First Mate reads the project instructions, tracker and current delivery posture before it accepts
or routes work. The evidence may make one ship Assignment immediately honest. It may instead show
that the organization needs one bounded fact, one human choice or another outcome first.

The original intent should remain recognizable while the route changes. AgentOS is preserving an
outcome, not executing a frozen graph.

## Distinguish four honest outcomes

Look for the smallest truthful next state:

1. **Assignment:** the work is actionable and one Agent owns returning the accepted evidence.
2. **Scout:** one bounded uncertainty needs investigation before ship work is honest.
3. **Captain decision:** a consequential choice exceeds the delegated authority and comes back to
   you with its complete options.
4. **Dependency:** another distinct outcome must land before this one can proceed.

A Scout is not a second implementation worker racing the owner. A dependency is not a vague
“blocked” label. Each state says what evidence or decision can change it.

## Check what became durable

A Task can preserve deliberate backlog, but a Task without an Assignment is not accepted
asynchronous work. Once execution is accepted, exactly one active Assignment names the accountable
owner.

New evidence may justify another Task, but only for a genuinely distinct outcome the organization
should preserve. Routine tool calls, implementation steps and raw reasoning stay in their native
session instead of becoming a shadow planning tree.

Ask First Mate:

> Show me the accepted Task, its active Assignment, any Scout, decision or dependency, and the
> native artifact that will prove delivery. Do not summarize activity as evidence.

## Know what good looks like

You can still identify the original outcome and authority boundary. Accepted work has one owner.
Uncertainty is explicit rather than hidden behind confident status. Every claim points to the
system that can prove it, and merge still belongs to you.

That is progressive planning: current evidence can change the route without losing intent,
responsibility or human control. Next, [watch and steer the
work](/learn/01-first-outcome/watch-and-steer) when a new fact makes an assumption stale.
