## Crew shape follows responsibility

Crew shape follows durable responsibility:

- First Mate performs Fleet coordination itself.
- A bounded Crewmate investigates or ships one project outcome.
- A Second Mate owns a continuing domain and forms its own Crewmate subtree.

Do not create a persistent domain merely to parallelize one Task. Do not keep a project change on
First Mate merely because it looks small or urgent.

## Bounded today, persistent later

The first onboarding validation change belongs to one repository and can be handled by a bounded
ship Crewmate. After several related outcomes, you may decide that “customer onboarding
reliability” is a continuing domain across two repositories.

First Mate then proposes a narrow charter:

> Own onboarding reliability for the web and identity repositories. Diagnose and deliver reviewed
> changes. Escalate production mutation, credentials, cross-product scope and merge.

If approved and supported by the release, First Mate provisions one Second Mate with a stable
identity and home. That Second Mate is idle when its queue is empty. It does not invent work.

Humans continue addressing First Mate or the company surface. Internal domain routing remains the
Fleet’s job.
