## The mental model

Visibility does not require a progress message every few minutes. Constant reporting consumes the
same human attention autonomy is supposed to return.

Use the authority that owns the question:

- PostgreSQL: who owns what, which decisions are open?
- Kubernetes: is the intended workload healthy?
- Herdr: what is the exact native session doing?
- Git/provider: what was committed, checked and opened for review?

First Mate supervises through durable reconciliation and narrow wake paths. It stays silent during
ordinary waiting and reports material outcomes.

## Worked example: inspect onboarding

The onboarding worker is active. First Mate reports no new decision because none exists. You want
detail, so you attach to the named Herdr Agent and see a compatibility test failing.

That terminal view gives direct process evidence. It does not mark an Inbox row read or change the
Assignment. If you give a correction in the attached terminal, the worker treats it as
authoritative human steering and reconciles the change durably.

Later, the provider pull request becomes the durable review artifact. You do not need First Mate to
copy every CI log into chat.

## What changes at this layer?

- Humans can inspect deeply without relaying every internal message.
- Sparse reporting focuses attention on outcomes and decisions.
- Native terminals remain available instead of being hidden by a dashboard.
- Observability stays separate from authority.
