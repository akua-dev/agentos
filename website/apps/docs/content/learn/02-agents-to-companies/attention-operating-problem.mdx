## Durable work, ephemeral wake

Organizations fail when everything interrupts everyone or when important work is only ephemeral.
AgentOS separates the durable communication from the signal that attention may be useful.

```text
commit Inbox row
  → targeted wake hint
  → recipient loads durable bearings
  → receives
  → reads
  → resolves only when the speech act permits it
```

PostgreSQL `LISTEN/NOTIFY` is a wake hint, not a queue. A disconnected Agent may miss it while the
row remains. After reconnecting, the Agent queries durable bearings.

## A decision survives a crash

The payments Second Mate sends the migration decision upward and commits the Inbox row. First Mate
wakes, presents the question and the Captain reads it. The terminal then crashes.

On recovery:

- the wake may be gone;
- receipt and read timestamps may exist;
- the decision is still unresolved;
- dependent work stays blocked.

Only the Captain’s exact answer resolves it. The system never equates “displayed” with “approved.”

A person talks to the company surface; First Mate routes internally. People do not maintain a map
of Mate handles or receive every worker update. Wake, load, read and resolve remain different
events because human attention is scarce organizational capacity.

source of truth per concern](/learn/02-agents-to-companies/one-source-of-truth).
