## The mental model

Organizations fail when everything interrupts everyone or when important work is only ephemeral.
AgentOS separates the durable communication from the signal that attention may be useful.

```text
commit Inbox row
  → targeted wake hint
  → recipient loads durable bearings
  → receives
  → reads
  → resolves only when the speech act permits it
```

PostgreSQL `LISTEN/NOTIFY` is a wake hint, not a queue. A disconnected Agent can miss the signal
while the row remains. After reconnecting, it queries what still needs attention.

## Worked example: decision delivery survives a crash

The payments Second Mate sends the migration decision upward and commits the Inbox row. First Mate
wakes, presents the question and the Captain reads it. The terminal then crashes.

On recovery:

- the wake may be gone;
- receipt and read timestamps may exist;
- the decision is still unresolved;
- dependent work stays blocked.

Only the Captain’s exact answer resolves it. The system never equates “displayed” with “approved.”

This model also protects human attention. A person talks to the company surface; First Mate routes
internally. People do not maintain a map of Second-Mate handles or receive every worker update.

## What changes at this layer?

- Important speech acts survive missed notifications and process loss.
- Wake, load, read and resolve have different evidence.
- Attention can be targeted through hierarchy instead of broadcast.
- Human judgment is treated as scarce organizational capacity.
