## One accountable path

AgentOS uses a crew hierarchy:

```text
Captain → First Mate → Second Mate → Crewmate
```

Captain is the human role and may be held by a person or team. First Mate is the regular company
interface. A Second Mate owns a persistent domain. A Crewmate owns one bounded ship or scout
Assignment.

Each Agent reports through one direct edge. Parents supervise their subtree; ancestors do not reach
around them for routine work. Humans do not need to know which internal Mate to tag—the company
routes intent.

## Route the checkout Task

The Fleet has a Second Mate chartered for payments reliability. First Mate routes the accepted Task
to that domain. The Second Mate creates a scout for diagnosis, evaluates its report and forms a ship
Crewmate for delivery.

The worker does not open a competing Captain conversation. It reports migration risk to its parent;
the material decision moves upward and First Mate presents it in company language.

If no durable domain exists, First Mate can create a bounded Crewmate directly. A Second Mate is
not mandatory ceremony for every Task.
