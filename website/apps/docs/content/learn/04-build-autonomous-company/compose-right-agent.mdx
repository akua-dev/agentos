## The mental model

An Agent’s composition is the smallest reviewed bundle that lets it own its outcome:

```text
identity instructions
+ conditional Skills
+ verified harness/model
+ native settings
+ required capabilities
+ evidence of the resolved bundle
```

Always-applicable role and safety belong in the nearest `AGENTS.md`. Conditional judgment belongs
in one Skill. Deterministic mechanics belong in TypeScript or SQL. The harness owns its native
settings instead of AgentOS flattening every option into a universal schema.

## Worked example: support engineering composition

The support-domain Second Mate needs:

- its narrow charter and hierarchy identity;
- delegation, diagnosis, decision and supervision Skills;
- read access to the approved support evidence boundary;
- project registry and provider capabilities for the web repository;
- a persistent verified Mate harness.

Its bounded diagnostic Crewmate needs a different composition: project instructions, read-heavy
source access and a report contract, but no source write or production capability. A later ship
Crewmate receives an isolated worktree and delivery tools.

More capabilities do not make an Agent better. They increase the consequences of a mistaken next
step and the burden of proving authority.

## What changes at this layer?

- Agent behavior becomes reviewable by concern.
- Persistent role and bounded outcome receive different bundles.
- Capability follows the outcome instead of a global super-agent.
- Harness portability is tested rather than assumed.
