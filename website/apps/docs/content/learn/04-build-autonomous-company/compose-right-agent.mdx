## Compose only what the outcome needs

An Agent’s composition is the smallest reviewed bundle that lets it own its outcome:

```text
identity instructions
+ conditional Skills
+ verified harness/model
+ native settings
+ required capabilities
+ evidence of the resolved bundle
```

Persistent Mate role and safety belong in the selected role instructions. Project-intrinsic rules
belong in the nearest `AGENTS.md`; conditional judgment belongs in one Skill; deterministic
mechanics belong in TypeScript or SQL. The harness owns its native settings instead of AgentOS
flattening every option into one schema.

## Support engineering needs two compositions

The support-domain Second Mate needs:

- its narrow charter and hierarchy identity;
- delegation, diagnosis, decision and supervision Skills;
- read access to the approved support evidence boundary;
- project registry and provider capabilities for the web repository;
- a persistent verified Mate harness.

Its diagnostic Crewmate needs project instructions, read-heavy source access and a report contract,
but no source-write or production capability. A later ship Crewmate receives an isolated worktree
and delivery tools.

More capabilities do not make an Agent better. They increase the consequences of a mistaken next
step and the burden of proving authority.
