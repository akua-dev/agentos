## People supervise outcomes

Human attention is part of the organizational operating system. A useful company surface lets
people express intent, see accountable progress, inspect evidence and answer consequential
questions without understanding the internal Agent topology.

The surface should route to First Mate. First Mate routes through one hierarchy edge to the right
domain. No Second-Mate tagging is required.

What reaches people:

- accepted outcomes and current accountable owner;
- material phase change, risk, blocker or failure;
- exact decisions only people can make;
- direct attach when they ask for detail.

Routine waits, internal handoffs and empty checks stay inside the Fleet.

## Present the consequence, not the topology

The support domain discovers a defect affected 18 accounts but the proposed query would expose
customer identifiers to a diagnostic worker. First Mate presents:

> Diagnosis can continue with aggregated evidence, or the support domain can receive temporary
> scoped access to the 18 affected records. The latter exposes customer identifiers. Which boundary
> do you choose?

The Captain team answers through the company surface. The exact decision reaches the dependent
Task; internal routing and wake re-arm remain invisible unless something fails.

Captain may be a team while decision scope remains exact. The internal hierarchy can evolve without
changing the human interface, and attention consumption becomes something the company can measure.
