## The mental model

Agent activity is not organizational performance. Count verified human outcomes and the human
attention, time, retries, tokens, duplication, recovery and risk used to deliver them.

Useful dimensions include:

- outcome effectiveness and acceptance evidence;
- Captain decisions, clarifications and repair interventions;
- elapsed time and tool/model consumption;
- duplicated work and retry behavior;
- interruption detection and return to useful work;
- authority, safety and chain of custody;
- missing telemetry and explicit limitations.

One composite score can hide failure. Publish the dimensions and the failed or incomplete attempts
that shaped improvement.

## Worked example: support-domain baseline

Run five declared support-to-review scenarios:

1. new reproducible defect;
2. duplicate provider delivery;
3. existing Task match;
4. controlled worker interruption;
5. consequence requiring a Captain decision.

For each, freeze the exact subject and evidence. Measure whether an accepted review artifact
exists, how many human turns were consumed, whether the wake path survived, and whether any access
or authority boundary was violated.

If token telemetry is missing, record `unknown`. Do not report zero or omit the dimension to make
the company appear efficient.

## What changes at this layer?

- Autonomy becomes evidence rather than demo theater.
- Human attention is treated as an explicit cost.
- Recovery and safety are first-class performance.
- The company can compare reviewed improvements over time.
