An autonomous company is a human-led, Agent-operated organization. People define purpose,
priorities, boundaries and consequential authority. Agents carry accepted outcomes through
execution, observation, recovery and evidence without making a human relay every internal step.

Autonomy is therefore not a binary feature and not permission to ignore consequences. A Fleet may
independently investigate a defect and prepare a reviewed pull request while still requiring a
Captain decision to merge, spend money, access credentials or mutate production.

## The company loop

1. People express an outcome through their chosen workflow.
2. The organization gives it durable identity and an accountable owner.
3. Agents execute through native tools and return evidence.
4. Reality—customers, production, providers and tests—changes the next plan.
5. Reviewed learning improves future behavior.

The loop becomes more autonomous only as narrow behavior earns wider standing authority. Native
permissions remain necessary but never prove that a human authorized a particular consequence.

AgentOS’s sovereignty principle matters here: if the orchestration surface disappears, the
organization’s durable state, workloads, homes and delivered code still live in standard systems
the operator controls.
