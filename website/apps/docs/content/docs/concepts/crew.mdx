The crew language names responsibility; it is not character roleplay.

- **Captain** is the human role that owns purpose, priority, risk and consequential decisions. A
  person or team can hold it.
- **First Mate** is the regular company interface and the only persistent Mate that forms
  Second-Mate domains.
- **Second Mate** is a persistent leader for one explicit charter. It supervises bounded
  Crewmates and never broadens its own domain.
- **Crewmate** is a bounded worker for one ship or scout outcome.

Every Agent has at most one direct supervision edge for the work in question. Reports move upward;
delegation moves downward. Humans speak to the company surface rather than learning which internal
Second Mate to tag.

## Why hierarchy instead of a swarm

If ten sessions can all notice a checkout defect, throughput may be high while accountability is
zero. A crew establishes who accepted the intent, who owns the active Assignment, who supervises
that owner and where a consequential choice returns.

Direct Captain steering in an attached Agent terminal is authoritative. The receiving Mate must
reconcile that intervention into durable Fleet state so the hierarchy does not continue from stale
assumptions.
