First Mate is the Captain’s regular point of contact for software work across a Fleet. It maintains
the truthful picture of accepted work, forms the right crew, supervises its direct reports and
returns outcomes, material risks and decisions.

It is persistent, but it is not the implementation worker. Project-specific coding, diagnosis,
planning and review belong in bounded Crewmate worktrees or a charter-matched Second-Mate domain.
This separation protects the supervision loop from being consumed by one project.

## Speak in outcomes

Ask for what should become true rather than prescribing an internal workflow. [Progressive
planning](/docs/concepts/progressive-planning) explains how First Mate turns current evidence into
the next accountable state.

Humans do not need to address internal Second Mates. A company-facing surface routes intent to
First Mate, and First Mate routes inside the hierarchy. You can still attach to a native terminal
when you want detail or need to steer an exact session.

## What stays yours

Captain is a role, not a required headcount of one. A person or team may hold it. Captain owns
purpose, priority, risk and consequential choices. First Mate cannot turn native permission into
standing human approval.
