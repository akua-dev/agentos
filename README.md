AgentOS is the organizational layer for persistent Agent teams. Bring the
models, harnesses, repositories, trackers and infrastructure you already trust;
AgentOS turns them into a crew with continuity, accountable ownership and a
verifiable path from human intent to delivered evidence.

[See what AgentOS is and where it fits →](https://agentos.akua.dev/docs/start/what-is-agentos)

## Stop being the message bus

One Agent in one session works at the price of your full attention. Add more
sessions and you become the infrastructure: switching tabs, carrying context,
checking progress and recovering abandoned work.

AgentOS gives accepted work one owner, preserves handoffs and returns progress,
risk and the decisions only people can make. Attach to any real terminal when
you want detail. Stay in control without staying in every loop.

[Learn how AgentOS protects organizational attention →](https://agentos.akua.dev/docs/concepts/organizational-attention)

## Get started

Copy this into the coding Agent you already use:

```text
Read https://github.com/akua-dev/agentos/blob/main/BOOTSTRAP.md.
Help me bring AgentOS online — check my setup first, ask before changing anything.
```

It inspects your environment, explains the real choices and asks before cost,
credentials, cluster creation, RBAC or installation. Then you meet First Mate:
a persistent Agent that keeps the truthful company view while work continues.

[Run your first outcome in AgentOS Learn →](https://agentos.akua.dev/learn)

## Start locally. Finish with the Fleet.

Plan, prototype, diagnose or partially implement with the local Agent where you
think fastest. When the result deserves durable ownership, hand First Mate the
desired outcome, evidence, artifact references, constraints and authority
boundary. Accepted work becomes a Task with one accountable Assignment; Fleet
evidence returns for your next local review or iteration.

[Learn the local-to-Fleet handoff workflow →](https://agentos.akua.dev/docs/operate/continue-local-work)

## Bring your own workplace

People should not have to accept one permanent Agent dashboard. A purpose-built
interface can prepare each employee's relevant decisions and context, present a
choice in the form that fits it, help revise feedback with a local Agent and
send only approved intent to First Mate.

[Flect](https://github.com/akua-dev/flect) is a public developer-preview
interface shell and a natural first AgentOS dogfooding adopter. The proposed
AgentOS experience is not a shipped integration: Flect remains useful for other
products and personal interfaces, while AgentOS remains the durable company
authority beneath any surface.

[Explore human work surfaces and the Flect concept →](https://agentos.akua.dev/docs/concepts/human-work-surfaces)

## Proof before promises

AgentOS publishes benchmark attempts—including failures—and links every result
to sanitized evidence.

| Public proof | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Quickstart to reviewed delivery | Five declared attempts: three passed, one failed and one ended incomplete. The final two passes delivered in **16m 59s** and **16m 16s**. |
| Human attention | The final two passes needed **zero** Captain follow-up turns, repair interventions or manual operational actions. |
| Interrupted-worker recovery | Accepted work resumed after controlled runtime loss with no lost changes, duplicate effects or human repair. |
| Portability | A native Codex-only run passed the same portable benchmark without AgentOS or a compatibility layer. |

[Inspect the evidence and evaluation method →](https://agentos.akua.dev/docs/reference/benchmarks)

## How the company works

You state an outcome. First Mate turns accepted intent into owned work. Second
Mates can lead durable domains; bounded Crewmates investigate or deliver. Human
authority remains explicit where consequences live. Results and real-world
signals return as evidence, decisions and reviewed learning—not notification
noise.

```text
Captain → First Mate → Second Mate → Crewmate
human intent → Task → Assignment → verified result
```

[Meet the crew and its accountability model →](https://agentos.akua.dev/docs/concepts/crew)

## Native systems. One organization.

AgentOS connects the authorities a software company already needs without
hiding them behind a universal wrapper or shadow world model.

| Concern | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Human planning and provider workflow | Your chosen tracker or provider |
| Accepted work, ownership and decisions | PostgreSQL |
| Live workloads and persistent homes | Kubernetes |
| Inspectable harness sessions | Herdr |
| Delivered source and review artifacts | Git and its remote |
